# EL item set 1 (STRUCTURE) — rework report

Scope: the 16 structure blocks of the NYC elevated kit — `el_column`,
`el_column_silver/_station/_named/_named_station`, `el_lattice_column{,_silver,
_station}`, `el_girder{,_silver,_station}`, `el_truss{,_silver,_station}`,
`el_deck_ties`, `el_deck_plate`. Everything is generated by
`tools/gen_el_assets.py`; the Java touched is `ElGirderBlock` and `ElDeckBlock`.
No new block ids, no registrations changed.

Renders: `el_set1_renders/` — `before_*.png` vs `after_*.png` of the same five
camera setups (`render_el_set1.py`, rerunnable; `EL_MODELS=<dir>` renders an
older model tree). Two audit scripts live beside them:
`audit_coplanar.py` (0 coplanar overlapping face pairs across the set) and
`audit_png.py` (every `el_*` texture valid, square, RGBA, IDAT length correct).

---

## 1. The table problem — what it actually was

`before_bent_side.png` is the diagnosis: a run of girders with a deck course on
top rendered as **one unbroken 2 m green apron on two sticks**. Three separate
causes, all now fixed:

1. **The girder had almost no relief.** Flanges were 1.2 px tall against a
   13.6 px web, and the only vertical members were the two panel stiffeners at
   the block ends — so 14 px of every block was flat sheet.
2. **The tie/plate deck's stringers were 12.8 px deep solid slabs** running the
   whole height of their block and sitting *outboard* of the girder's flange.
   Stacked on a girder that added a second full-block-deep wall flush with the
   first — that is what doubled the apron.
3. **The knee braces were invisible.** They were 45°-rotated struts aimed *into*
   the column: every part of them with `4.7 <= x <= 11.3` was buried inside the
   column body, leaving about 4 px of visible stub per brace. The signature el
   silhouette was effectively missing.

### What changed

**Girder (`girder_elements`)** — real plate-girder section:

| member | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| flange | y 0..1.2 / 14.8..16, z 5.5..10.5 | y 0..2 / 14..16, z 4.6..11.4 |
| web | z 6.7..9.3 (1.2 px recess) | z 7.0..9.0 (**2.2 px recess** each side) |
| stiffeners | 2, at the block ends only | **4** at x 0.05 / 5.15 / 9.65 / 14.75 |
| stiffener relief | 0.4 px proud of the web | **1.2 px** proud |

Flange edges now sample the sheet's horizontal rivet seam (`uv v 3..4`) so a
rivet row runs the length of every flange; stiffeners sample the vertical rivet
ladder (`u 13.5..14.5`). Both matter more than the geometry: **Minecraft shades
every north-facing quad identically**, so relief on a facing wall only reads
through the texture window a face samples and through AO. The renderer was
extended to tint the riveted uv windows for exactly this reason.

**Truss (`truss_elements`)** — now genuinely open. Chords are 2.2 px deep
angle pairs (were 2.6 px chords floating 0.5 px off the block edges, leaving a
gap against a neighbour above/below); the lacing web is 11.6 px tall (was 10.2)
with three vertical posts instead of two heavy end posts. `tex_lattice` was
rebuilt at **32 px**: 2-texel straps on an 8-texel pitch = 1 model px (~6 cm)
lacing bars at a 4 px (25 cm) pitch, half the bar width and twice the diamond
count of the old 16 px sheet, which is what made lattice columns and trusses
read as a dark panel. Opacity is 43.8 % either way — the difference is entirely
grain. (`after_truss_close.png`, `after_lattice_bent.png`.)

**Knee braces (`brace_elements`)** — replaced with **stepped gusset plates**
that live in the *air beside* the column (the column below occupies x 4.7..11.3;
x 0..4.7 and 11.3..16 are empty). Six steps drop 9 px down the column shaft and
taper to nothing under the girder, dying 0.6 px into the shaft. Axis-aligned
boxes only, so the blockstate's y-rotation is exact and nothing is at a
non-standard angle. Steps abut rather than overlap and each omits the face on
its deeper side (entirely buried in the next step) — that is what keeps the
plate free of coplanar overlapping faces. `after_brace_close.png` /
`after_bent_side.png` show the flare that was missing.

**Decks (`stringer_elements`)** — the stringers still land on the girder (they
reach y 0.1, not y 0: the girder's top-flange face is exactly on y 16 and two
cull-tagged faces on the same plane z-fight), but they are now **8 px deep
riveted I-beams** — proud top and bottom flanges, recessed web — instead of
12.8 px slabs, and their flanges are inset 0.3 px *inside* the girder's flange
below so the girder keeps the proud line of the whole assembly. The 4.8 px
below them is **open**, bridged only by a bearing block at each block end;
across a joint those pair into the transverse floor beam a real el has at every
panel point. That open band is the single biggest reason the run stopped
reading as a table (`after_bent_street.png`, `after_bent_3q.png`).

Tie deck ties went from 2.4 px wide / 1.6 px gaps to **2.0 / 2.0** on the same
4 px pitch — half the deck is daylight from the street, and the ties cantilever
~3 px past the stringers, as the real ones do.

**Columns** — the foot was a two-step taper that read as a table-leg flare and
the cap was the same taper upside down. Both are now three squared steps with
different proportions: the foot is a wide bolted **base plate** (11.6 px = 72 cm),
a bolster and a shoe collar; the cap is a collar, a bolster and a flatter, wider
**bearing plate** (11.8 px) for the girder to sit on. Base and cap plates sample
the rivet seam so their anchor bolts show. The cap plate stops at y 15.9 — the
girder's bottom flange face is on y 16 and both carried a cullface, i.e. a
latent z-fight at every bent.

## 2. Orientation audit — placement rule per block

Read together: `getPlacementState`, the blockstate's rotation entry, and the
model's authored frame.

| block | property source | model frame | blockstate | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `el_girder{,_silver,_station}` | `AXIS = context.getHorizontalPlayerFacing().getAxis()` | authored along **+X**, section centred on z 8 | `axis=x` → as authored; `axis=z` → `y:90` | **correct.** A builder standing on the track line looking along it gets a girder along the track. |
| `el_truss*` | same class (`ElGirderBlock`) | authored along +X | same | **correct**, and it shares the girder's brace model so a mixed run braces identically. |
| `el_girder*` / `el_truss*` `BRACED` | `pos.down()` is an el column | brace authored along +X, mirrored about x 8 | added by the `braced=true` multipart part, rotated with the axis | **was wrong, fixed.** The old test was `instanceof ColumnBlock`, which is also true of the iron platform column and the slim canopy / entrance-portal posts (2.8 px shafts) — those grew gussets cut for a 6.6 px shaft, reaching well past the post. Now an explicit list of the eight el columns. |
| `el_deck_ties`, `el_deck_plate` | `AXIS = getHorizontalPlayerFacing().getAxis()` | **stringers along +X**, ties across (spanning z) | `variants axis=x` / `axis=z` `y:90` | **correct.** AXIS is the direction of travel; stringers follow it and ties cross it, matching the girder placed with the same gesture. |
| `el_column`, `el_column_silver`, `el_column_station`, `el_lattice_column{,_silver,_station}` | `FacingDecorBlock`: `FACING = getHorizontalPlayerFacing().getOpposite()`; `UP`/`DOWN` from vertical `ColumnBlock` neighbours | 4-fold symmetric | multipart keys **`up`/`down` only** — `facing` is never read | **correct / no-op.** FACING is carried but invisible; the shaft, foot and cap are symmetric. |
| `el_column_named`, `el_column_named_station` | as above (`StationColumnBlock`) | as above + a BER name board at `boardOffset` 0.1875 | same | **correct** — FACING is the opposite of the player's look, so the board faces whoever placed it (the iron-column precedent). |

Two things found but deliberately **not** changed, because they are outside this
set's files:

- `ColumnBlock.withConnections` counts *any* `ColumnBlock` above/below, so an
  el column stacked directly on a canopy post or an iron column suppresses its
  cap/foot. Cross-family stacking is nonsense in practice; fixing it means
  editing the shared `ColumnBlock`, which other sets ride on.
- `el_column*` outline is 4.7..11.3, so the new base and cap plates (11.6 /
  11.8 px) stand slightly outside the selection box. The old foot did too.

Also fixed in `ElDeckBlock`:

- `getOutlineShape` returned the **top slab only** while the model runs the full
  height of the block; it is now the full cube. Collision is unchanged (top slab,
  so you walk on the deck and nobody bumps an invisible wall underneath).
- `isSideInvisible` culled against *any* `ElDeckBlock`, so a plate deck beside a
  tie deck culled its slab's cullface-tagged side faces and left a hole. It now
  requires the **same block** (the vanilla glass rule).
- `ElGirderBlock`'s outline shape was z 5..11 against a model that is now
  4.6..11.4; updated to match.

## 4. Proposed, NOT built (needs new block ids — your call)

1. **`el_cross_girder`** — the transverse bent cap. Right now a bent is only
   implied: two independent column stacks under a longitudinal girder. The
   photos' defining member is the cross girder *between* the two columns of a
   bent, with knee braces flaring down both. A single AXIS block that braces on
   `pos.down()` like `ElGirderBlock` would do it, and stacking it under the
   longitudinal girder gives a true two-way bent.
2. **`el_x_brace`** — the X-brace panel between a bent's two columns (Marcy Av,
   125 St). One block wide, two 45° straps plus a horizontal strut, cutout; place
   a column of them between two column stacks.
3. **`el_deck_ties_open`** — a tie deck with *no* stringers at all, for spans
   that sit directly on a cross girder or between two longitudinal girders.
   Would let a builder choose between "deck carries itself" and "deck rides a
   girder" instead of always paying for the stringers.
4. **`el_girder_end`** — a girder block whose free end closes with a bearing
   shoe/end plate rather than an open section. Today a run's last block shows the
   web's open end (it has no east/west faces, by design, so runs join cleanly).
5. **`el_catwalk`** — the emergency walkway with pipe handrail alongside the
   deck; would sit naturally beside `el_deck_ties`.
6. **125 St arch braces** — already on the plan's "still unbuilt" list; they need
   45°/22.5° chord pieces rather than a new mechanism.

## 5. Not verified

Nothing was run in game (no Gradle, no client, per the brief). Everything above
is offline: the generator's own `verify()` (blockstate when-keys against the
Java properties, uv on every face, uv on the sprite, rotation angles), the
coplanar audit, the PNG audit and the isometric renders. Placement flows
(`getPlacementState` for AXIS and the braced/UP/DOWN detection), tint providers
on the `_station` variants, and the cutout layer on the lattice column and truss
still need a real client.
