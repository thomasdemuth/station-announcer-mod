Station Announcer · manual driving overhaul · round 3

Cab HUD

Drawn the way the mod will draw it: real GUI pixels, Minecraft-style pixel text with its drop shadow, flat fills and small sprites. Rows keep their place and grey out when they don't apply. Every panel collapses. The run panel starts collapsed, showing only the next two stops.

Situation
Panels

Click any panel to expand or collapse it. In game: a "HUD detail" key cycles them, or set each one in the HUD layout screen.

What's on screen

  • Top left, destination. Line bullet and destination; under it the next stop and its distance. At a platform the arrow becomes a stop mark. Expanded adds the stop after.
  • Bottom left, signal and speed. Signal head and its distance, speed, current limit, next limit and where it starts. Expanded adds three fixed rows: stop marker (grey until you're braking for a platform), power/brake, and lamps (doors, guard ready, hold).
  • Bottom right, run. How early or late you are, the clock, the next two stops (scheduled → expected) and the departure timer, which stays in place and greys out between stations. Expanded lists the whole run.
  • Top centre, events. One short line, only while something needs you.
  • Bottom panels grow upward from a fixed bottom edge, so collapsing never moves the rows you're reading.
